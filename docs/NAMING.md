## Ranked top 5

All five returned 404 on every TLD checked, so each should be a **standard registration**. Registry premiums can't be seen through RDAP, so confirm the price at checkout.

| Rank | Name | Rationale | Approx. price tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Full Ballot** (fullballot.com) | Two plain words that are easy to say and spell, and it promises the core value: every race, not just the top of the ticket. Nonpartisan and durable across elections. | Standard (.com about $10–15/yr, .vote about $20–40/yr) |
| 2 | **See My Ballot** (seemyballot.com) | Most literal: it is exactly the user's intent and the product's action. Very searchable. The risk is that it reads as a feature rather than a brand. | Standard |
| 3 | **Ballot Preview** (ballotpreview.com) | Clear that you're looking before you vote. Easy over the phone. Slightly generic, and "preview" sounds temporary for a tool meant to last. | Standard |
| 4 | **Ballot Primer** (ballotprimer.com) | Says "explainer" and suits the neutral-education stance. Ambiguous pronunciation costs it on the phone test. | Standard |
| 5 | **Fill the Bubble** (fillthebubble.com) | The most memorable and friendly option. Longer, with a lighter tone, so it may undercut a "trustworthy civic tool" positioning. | Standard |

**On keeping "BallotHelper":**
- .org, .vote and .us are free.
- .com is held by someone else with no site, and expires 2026-12-03.
- Launching on ballothelper.org is viable. But "BallotHelper" sits close to the new same-category entrants (Ballot Explained, Plain Ballot, Read My Ballot), and "Full Ballot" is more distinct and owns every TLD.

**Next steps for the chosen name:**
1. Run a USPTO search (Class 9/35/42/45) and a web search.
2. Register .com, .org and .vote together. .app is optional; it forces HTTPS, which is fine on Vercel.
